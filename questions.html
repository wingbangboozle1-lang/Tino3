What is known, what is missing, and how to find it.

How to use this page

There are 33 questions below, grouped by subject. Each one states what this site already has, what is missing, how to find the answer, and why it changes the decision. Questions with no official source in the last column are matters of fact or preference that no public source can answer; they are listed anyway so nothing is assumed away.

Every item here is also a candidate for the suggestions page, which is the review queue for turning them into action.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.
