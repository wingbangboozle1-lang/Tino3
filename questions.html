Questions before deciding

A clear list of questions, information and answers that need to be addressed before any decision can be reasonably made, and how to discover missing information.

Keep answers and supporting documents in a private working file. The public cells below remain blank; no answer is requested or stored by this site. The list distinguishes what the public guide can answer from official sources and what must be verified privately.

Suggestions to discover missing information

  1. Request current assessor roll and tax bill for each property and ask the assessor for written eligibility guidance before relying on any exclusion.
  2. Obtain a qualified date-of-death valuation and keep the appraisal with estate records for basis support.
  3. Reconcile rent ledgers, leases, deposits and vacancy month by month for B; do the same prospectively for any rent of A.
  4. Collect 12 months of actual water, electricity/solar, insurance and maintenance invoices; separate routine maintenance from capital work with contractor estimates.
  5. Contact each retirement custodian for the latest beneficiary designation and plan distribution rules; do not assume a ten-year or spouse rule without confirmation.
  6. Have a licensed professional review title, trust terms, tenant notices, coverage and local ordinances in private.
  7. Document decision criteria and then test each path under the irregular economic cases rather than a single growth rate.
Privacy reminder. Do not paste deeds, tax bills, account numbers, exact assessments, quotes or other personal details into a public issue, pull request or website field. Keep them in the private workbook described on Suggestions for review.
