The short version

  • Answered: the governing tax rules, the reassessment mechanics, the deadlines after a death, and which option wins on current numbers — sell Property A, reinvest, keep Property B.
  • Assumed: growth, inflation, returns, market rent and premiums, each labelled and stress-tested on the decision and black-swan pages.
  • Missing: title, trust terms, the estate inventory, real quotes and the solar end date (Q-01 to Q-11). Each has a named document that settles it; none requires publishing anything private.
