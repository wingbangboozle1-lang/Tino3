What we know, what we do not, and how to find out

Every question that has to be answered before the decision can be made, with its status, the official rule behind it, and the step that closes it.

What this page is. 39 questions, in the order they have to be answered. Each one states what is settled today and links the official page that settles it, what is still missing, the concrete step that would find it, and what changes if the answer comes back differently. Nothing here is inferred: where a household fact is unknown, the answer says so and the figure stays blank. Questions marked Your choice need a decision rather than research.

If you do only three things

  1. Read the trust and the deed (Q-07, Q-08). These two documents decide whether the sale is nearly tax-free and whether the rental building is reassessed. One afternoon with a scanner.
  2. Get three insurance quotes (Q-18). Homeowners with wildfire cover for Property A, landlord cover for Property B. Then the running-cost tables stop relying on a guess.
  3. Write down the cash total (Q-05). One number converts the federal room on the tax page into a tax bill or a zero.

What cannot be known now

Some of the most important inputs do not exist yet, and no amount of research will produce them early: the value of the home on the date of death, the exclusion amount in that year, the law in that year, the stock and house market of the following decade, and the family's circumstances. The plan handles them the only way that is honest: it shows a range, states the break-even point at which the answer changes, and re-runs itself each year. Anything else would be a forecast dressed up as a fact.
