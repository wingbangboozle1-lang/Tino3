Answered by the law — no further input needed

These questions are settled on the publishers' own pages and do not need the owner to supply anything. Each answer is read, not inferred; the source library holds the words relied on.

Questions the law already answers
No.QuestionAnswerBasisOfficial source
QA-01Who pays the federal estate tax?The estate, on the whole taxable estate, not the beneficiary on what he or she receives.VerifiedIRS · Form 706 instructions
QA-02Is there a California estate or inheritance tax?No. The state may not impose one, and none has been filed for deaths since 1 January 2005.VerifiedCA code · RTC 13301
QA-03Who is the taxpayer when the estate tax is charged?The estate: the tax is levied on the entire taxable estate, not on the share received by a particular beneficiary.VerifiedIRS · Form 706 instructions
QA-04What is the tax basis of the inherited home?Fair market value at the date of death, so a prompt sale produces almost no taxable gain.VerifiedIRS · Inherited basis
QA-05When is Form 706 due?Nine months after the death; a six-month extension can be requested on Form 4768, and a portability election depends on filing on time.VerifiedIRS · Form 706 instructions
QA-06How much can pass free of federal estate tax?$15,000,000 for a death in 2026 and $15,480,000 for a death in 2027. Later years are indexed and unknown.VerifiedIRS · 2027 amounts
QA-07How is the inherited home reassessed?At market value, reduced by the parent-child exclusion only if it was the parent's home, becomes the heir's home within one year, and both claims are filed on time.VerifiedCA code · RTC 63.2
QA-08How much can the assessed value rise in a year?By the change in the state price index, capped at 2%. The cap has bound in every year since 2022.VerifiedBOE · Letter 2026/002
QA-09Is Treasury interest taxed by the state?No. Treasury obligations and their interest are exempt from state and local tax, but not from estate or inheritance tax.VerifiedUS Code (GPO) · 31 USC 3124
QA-10How much can rent rise for a sitting tenant?1.6% from 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027, once every 12 months, with a rent-increase licence; a new tenancy has no limit.VerifiedCity B Rent Board · Rent increases

The short version

  • Answered by law: who pays the estate tax, that California has none, the basis reset, the Form 706 deadline, the exclusion amounts, the reassessment rules, the 2% cap, the Treasury exemption and the rent cap. See QA-01 to QA-10.
  • Answered by arithmetic: title aside, the ranking survives a stock return down to 4.7%, house growth up to 7.0%, insurance at three times the guess, a 1% state surcharge, and a return to the pre-2021 inheritance rules.
  • Needs a document: title, trust terms, itemised bills, quotes, the estate inventory, the solar tariff and the rent survey (Q-01 to Q-11; D-01 to D-09).
  • Cannot be known now: future tax law, future prices and future rates. Each is stress-tested on the two black-swan pages, and the choice of path is made reversible where possible.
