Questions before deciding

A short, ordered list of facts and documents needed before comparing the inherited property with the already-owned property.

Keep answers and supporting documents in a private working file. The public cells below remain blank; no answer is requested or stored by this site.

Resolve these questions first

Private question register — blank until verified
OrderQuestionPublic answerHow to resolve
1 · OwnershipWho holds legal and beneficial title to A and B, and what transfers on death?Not publishedRecorded title, trust/will, beneficiary and estate records
2 · TimingWhat event/date triggers notice, assessor, tax and claim deadlines?Not publishedDeath/transfer documents and official notices; see Inheritance steps
3 · A's basisWhat is A's defensible date-of-death value and basis record?Not publishedIndependent valuation and estate records; IRS inherited-basis rule
4 · Property taxDoes A qualify for a family-home exclusion, and were the occupancy/exemption/claim steps met?Not publishedCurrent statute and assessor decision; RTC section 63.2
5 · B's incomeWhat rent was collected, what was vacant, and what expenses/depreciation are recorded?Not publishedLeases, rent ledger, bills and prior returns; IRS Publication 527
6 · OccupancyWould the recipient use A or B as a home, and what rent or housing cost would change?Not publishedPersonal housing plan, tenant/lease review and current costs
7 · Running costsWhat are property tax, insurance, water, electricity/solar, maintenance and repair costs?Not publishedActual bills, policy/utility agreements, inspections and estimates
8 · Local lawWhich local rental, transfer-tax, property-tax and utility rules apply?Not publishedVerify jurisdiction privately; check assessor, local ordinance and utility/provider records
9 · Estate taxWhat are the estate's total assets, deductions, prior gifts and elections?Not publishedExecutor's records and current Form 706 instructions; IRS estate-tax rules
10 · Retirement accountsAre there retirement accounts or plans, who is designated as beneficiary, and what distribution rules apply?Not publishedLatest beneficiary designation and current plan/IRS rules; IRS beneficiary rules
11 · Risk and prioritiesWhat liquidity, workload, market, insurance and family-use risks matter most?Not publishedAgree decision criteria before choosing assumptions
