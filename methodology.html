Methodology - How Every Number Was Produced

Formulas (all money rounded to $10)

  • Assessed (no transfer): assessed(t) = assessed(2026) x 1.02^(t-2026) (Prop 13 cap, BOE).
  • Market (assumed): market(t) = market(2026) x (1+g)^(t-2026), g = 2%, 3%, or 4% - assumptions.
  • Prop 19 move-in assessed: factored base + max(0, market - factored base - $1,000,000).
  • Full reassessment (rent/sell): assessed = market.
  • Property tax: assessed x effective rate (Cupertino 1.335%, SF 2.135% user-implied; nominal sensitivities 1.20% / 1.18%).
  • Carrying cost: tax + insurance benchmark + water + electric + maintenance assumption.
  • Break-even rent: total annual cost / 12.
  • Sell & reinvest: net = market x (1 - 6% selling costs); future = net x (1+r)^n, r = 4%, 7%, or 10% - assumptions.

Limitations

  • Income tax on rent/gains excluded from net figures (bracket-dependent).
  • No vacancy, management-fee, or major-capital-expenditure (roof/seismic) allowance beyond the maintenance assumption.
  • Laws as of Nov 2026; exemption indexed from 2027; future legislatures can change any rule.
