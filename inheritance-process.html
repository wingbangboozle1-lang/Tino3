Step-by-step: trust inheritance (Cupertino + SF + cash)

  1. Confirm the trust is funded (before death)

    Both houses' titles are in the trust name; cash/brokerage accounts are titled to the trust or have TOD/POD designations. Unfunded assets fall back to probate. Verify with the trustee and title company. FUNDING STATUS UNVERIFIED — confirm before 2040.

  2. Death occurs; successor trustee steps in

    Trustee obtains certified death certificates (order 10–15 copies). No court petition needed. Trustee notifies beneficiaries and creditors per trust terms and CA Probate Code §§16060–16069.

  3. Secure and inventory assets

    Change locks, secure both houses, notify insurers (HO-3 / landlord policy), forward mail, inventory personal property, photograph condition. Order appraisals for both houses as of date of death — these set the step-up basis (FMV at death).

  4. File federal estate tax return if required (Form 706)

    Required only if gross estate + adjusted taxable gifts exceed the exemption for the year of death ($15,000,000 in 2026). Modeled estate ($4,900,000 houses + unknown cash) likely does not require filing unless cash exceeds ~$10,100,000. File anyway to elect portability if married. Due 9 months after death. IRS — Estate tax

  5. California: no estate/inheritance tax filing

    California has no estate or inheritance tax. No state filing. BOE

  6. Decide Cupertino path within 1 year (Prop 19 clock starts)

    If claiming the move-in exclusion: heir must establish primary residence and file homeowners' exemption (BOE-266) within 1 year of transfer, plus BOE-19-P within 3 years. If renting or selling: no filing needed, but full reassessment applies. Calendar both deadlines on day one. BOE — Prop 19

  7. Retitle / distribute per trust terms

    Trustee executes deeds transferring houses to beneficiaries (or holds for sale). Record with Santa Clara County (Cupertino) and SF Assessor-Recorder. Notify county assessors of change in ownership; expect supplemental tax bills 3–6 months after transfer.

  8. Pay debts, taxes, and administration expenses

    Trustee pays mortgage (if any — balance unknown, flagged), final income taxes, and expenses from estate cash. Mortgage balance: UNVERIFIED (not provided).

  9. Distribute cash inheritance

    Cash passes income-tax-free to beneficiaries (inheritance is not income). Only post-inheritance earnings are taxable. Document basis for any inherited securities (step-up applies to stocks too).

  10. Close out: final accounting + tax returns

    Trustee provides final accounting, files final fiduciary returns (Form 1041 if required), and distributes remainder. Keep appraisals, closing statements, and BOE filings permanently — they prove basis and Prop 19 compliance.
