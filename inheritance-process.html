How the inheritance actually works

Twelve steps from the death to a decision. Three of them carry deadlines with penalties, and one of them — the valuation date — fixes both your income-tax basis and the county's new assessed value.

The two dates that matter most

The date of death is simultaneously your basis in the property (fair market value, IRS basis FAQ↗) and the date the county reassesses it (SCC Assessor 2026↗). The 150th day after death is when the change in ownership statement is due (RTC 480↗); missing the assessor's request for it carries a penalty of the greater of $100 or 10% of the new tax, capped at $5,000.

Timing assumes the property is held in a trust, which is the premise of this project. Probate is not modelled because a trust avoids it for trust assets.
