Tax and law watch

A source-linked overview of current federal and California rules, enacted changes, pending proposals and local questions that remain unresolved.

As-of date: November 12, 2026. Tax thresholds and statutes change. Confirm the applicable law and professional advice at the actual transfer, filing and decision dates; no local jurisdiction is identified in this public guide.

General tax and property rules
AreaGeneral rule summaryOfficial source
Federal estate taxFor 2026, the basic exclusion is $15,000,000. For 2027 deaths, the basic exclusion is $15,480,000. The estate-tax filing test depends on the gross estate and adjusted taxable gifts; portability for a surviving spouse has a timely-return requirement. Estate tax, if due, is the estate's liability, separate from the beneficiary's income tax.Internal Revenue Service — Estate tax
Internal Revenue Service — What's new — Estate and gift tax
Internal Revenue Service — Tax inflation adjustments for tax year 2027
Internal Revenue Service — Instructions for Form 706
Receiving inherited propertyThe value of property acquired by inheritance is generally excluded from federal gross income; income later produced by that property is not excluded by this rule.U.S. Government Publishing Office (GovInfo) — 26 U.S.C. 102: gifts and inheritances (United States Code, 2022 edition)
Federal giftsThe 2027 annual gift exclusion is $20,000 per recipient. Gift and estate rules interact; do not treat the annual exclusion as the full lifetime estate-tax exclusion.Internal Revenue Service — Tax inflation adjustments for tax year 2027
Inherited basis and saleInherited-property basis is generally date-of-death fair market value, subject to exceptions and reporting rules. A later gain may be taxable; do not assume an inherited sale is tax-free.Internal Revenue Service — Gifts and inheritances: inherited property basis and sale
California income taxThe inheritance itself is not included in California income, but income later produced by inherited property may be taxable.California Franchise Tax Board — Gifts and inheritance
California estate taxThe State Controller says a California estate-tax return is not required for decedents dying on or after January 1, 2005; federal filing rules still apply.California State Controller's Office — California estate tax
California property taxThe general Proposition 13 framework has a 1% base rate plus voter-approved indebtedness and generally limits annual assessed-value increases to 2%; changes in ownership, new construction and local levies matter.California State Board of Equalization — California Property Tax: An Overview, Publication 29
Family-home reassessment exclusionA qualifying intergenerational family-home transfer has relationship, occupancy, exemption, value-cap and filing conditions. A principal-residence occupancy and homeowner/disabled-veteran exemption are generally required within one year.California Legislative Information — Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2
Rental income and expensesRental income and expenses are reported under federal tax rules. Common expense categories include insurance, taxes, repairs and utilities, but personal use, capital improvements and limitations affect treatment.Internal Revenue Service — Publication 527: Residential Rental Property
Statewide rent limitsFor covered California residential units, the annual rent-increase cap is generally 5% plus the applicable cost-of-living change or 10%, whichever is lower. Exceptions and local protections apply; the current statute states an end date of January 1, 2030.California Legislative Information — Civil Code section 1947.12: statewide rent limits and exceptions
Just-cause tenancy protectionsFor covered tenancies after 12 months of continuous lawful occupancy, the owner generally must state just cause in a written termination notice. A no-fault termination requires either one month's rent in relocation assistance within 15 calendar days or a written waiver of final-month rent before it is due; exemptions and local law matter.California Legislative Information — Civil Code section 1946.2: just cause to terminate a residential tenancy
Local transfer taxCalifornia law authorizes county and qualifying city documentary transfer taxes by local ordinance. A rate cannot be determined without the applicable jurisdiction and transaction facts.California Legislative Information — Revenue and Taxation Code section 11911: documentary transfer tax authorization
InsuranceState premium surveys are comparison tools, not individual insurance quotes. Confirm coverage, use and price directly with an insurer.California Department of Insurance — Compare insurance premiums
Solar / electricityExport credits depend on the utility, tariff and system/interconnection facts. Check the actual bill and system agreement.California Public Utilities Commission — Net energy metering and net billing
