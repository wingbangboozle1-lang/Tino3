Inheritance planning: verified research supplement

Official-source facts and limitations · reviewed 10 November 2026

Executive summary

No reliable winner can be established from the available evidence. Occupying a qualifying inherited family home may preserve part of its assessed value; renting or selling can change eligibility. A separate available home may reduce the need for replacement housing, but its ownership, rent and costs are not independently verified. Compare all three choices after obtaining professional, property-specific advice. No personal financial data is published in this supplement.

Future prices, insurance, returns, taxes and legal rules are unknown. Blank fields below are not zero. This is educational information, not legal or tax advice.
