Every citation on this site resolves to an entry in this register. Each entry states, narrowly, what the source supports — not a summary of the document, but the specific proposition relied on.

Verification standard

Verified means the page or document was read directly from the publisher on 2026-11-10 and the quoted rule, rate or figure appears in it. Anything that could not be matched is on the Flagged for review page and is not presented as fact anywhere else on this site. Owner-supplied figures are labelled reported and are never described as verified. Modelling inputs are labelled assumption.

Sources deliberately not used

  • Automated valuation estimates (Zillow, Redfin and similar). They are not official, they carry error bands that are large relative to the tax differences being discussed, and they cannot be cited for an assessed value.
  • Blog posts and law-firm marketing pages describing Prop 19. Several were read while locating the underlying rules; where they agreed with the Board of Equalization, the Board's own guidance is cited instead.
  • County assessor parcel records. Not used, because publishing a parcel identifier would compromise the anonymity requirement.

How to re-verify this site
