The solar credit is not a permanent feature

The owner reports that the array usually produces a net credit of roughly $10–$20 a month. That depends entirely on which net-metering tariff the system is on. California has moved newer interconnections to a different tariff structure with much lower export compensation. A system interconnected years ago may keep its original tariff, but the terms are not transferable in every case and change with the utility's filed rates. The model therefore treats the credit as a small offset in the near term and does not compound it. Flagged item 5 →
