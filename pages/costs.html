Why the solar “profit” needs a second look

Under the CPUC Net Billing Tariff (NBT) — in force for new interconnections since April 15, 2023 — exported solar is credited at its avoided-cost value to the grid, which is usually well below the retail rate you pay for imports (CPUC). A system interconnected before April 15, 2023 generally remains on legacy NEM 2.0 (retail-rate credits) for its 20-year transition period. The family-reported ~$10–$20/mo net credit is plausible under NEM 2.0 with high self-consumption, but cannot be confirmed without the system's Permission-to-Operate date and 12 months of bills. Modelled treatment: $0 net electricity cost for Property A (conservative), with the credit treated as upside — see Methodology.
