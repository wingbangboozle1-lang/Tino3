3. Recommendation (modelled)

  1. Retain Property B as rental + owner-occupied unit. It already produces ~$85,800/yr gross rent (family-provided) and the third unit lets you live there without losing income. Modelled net yield stays positive even after Prop 19 reassessment — but reassessment will raise its tax bill materially (see Properties).
  2. For Property A, decide by occupancy intent:
    • If you will live in Cupertino: occupy within 1 year, file homeowners' exemption + BOE-19-P. Modelled tax rises from ~$7,210 to ~$27,000–$30,000/yr at 2026 values (partial reassessment) — still far below renting it out.
    • If you will rent Property A: full reassessment to market value. Modelled tax ~$41,000/yr at 2026 values. Rental must clear this + insurance + maintenance to beat selling.
    • If you will sell Property A: step-up makes a prompt sale nearly gain-free. Reinvest net proceeds; modelled break-even vs. renting is ~5–7% after-tax return (see Decision Analysis).
  3. Do not gift appreciated property during life (carryover basis, IRC §1015) — inherit at death for step-up (IRC §1014). Keep the trust funded and the Prop 19 filings calendared (1-yr / 3-yr deadlines).
