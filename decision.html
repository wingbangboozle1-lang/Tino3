EVIDENCE-FIRST PLANNING

Sell, rent or live in A?

This is a decision rule, not a fabricated winner. B remains separately owned in every branch of the comparison.

Compare on equal terms

A sale has immediate liquidity; a rental has operating and tail risk; personal use has housing value. Apply the same 2040–2050 date, tax law and risk allowance.

Avoid the tempting error

Do not subtract B's entire market value from the other person's estate and simultaneously claim a new B basis: B is already held. Conversely, do not assume A's low historical assessed value survives a rental transfer. Verify title and trust before deciding.
