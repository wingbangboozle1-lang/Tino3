Choose criteria before looking at a winner

  • Financial: after-tax cash flow, liquidity, one-time costs and sensitivity to alternative returns.
  • Housing: whether either property is needed as a home, timing, accessibility and the cost of housing elsewhere.
  • Risk: vacancy, repair, insurance availability, local tax/rental law and market drawdowns.
  • Workload: management time, tenant obligations and the ability to fund unexpected work.
  • Values: family continuity, flexibility, debt reduction, diversification or a planned transfer.

Record which criteria matter most before selecting assumptions. A change to occupancy, title or local law can change the comparison; see Questions first.
