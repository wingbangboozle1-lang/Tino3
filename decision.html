4. Recommendation

  1. SF building: KEEP as rental + owner unit. Net ~$58,880/yr modeled. Do not transfer it as a rental (reassessment would cut net to ~$22,890/yr). Confirm rent-control status and keep tenancies compliant.
  2. Cupertino: LIVE IN if you need a primary residence; SELL if you don't.
    • Live in saves $13,940/yr vs. renting (tax $27,450 vs. $41,390) and provides housing. Must move in within 1 year + file BOE-19-P within 3 years.
    • Sell wins on pure wealth (+$1,566,140 over 10 yrs vs. holding, gross) and captures step-up (~$0 capital gains if prompt). Best if the family already has housing (e.g., SF owner unit) and wants liquidity.
    • Rent is last choice unless rent comps exceed ~$5,650/mo. It carries the highest tax ($41,390/yr) plus landlord costs and SF-style tenant-law complexity (Cupertino/Santa Clara rules differ — verify).
  3. Trust both houses now. Saves ~$124,000 in probate fees + 12 months. See Inheritance Steps.
  4. Estate tax: no action needed unless cash inheritance exceeds ~$13,782,920 (2040, single) — provide the cash figure to finalize.

This recommendation follows directly from the modeled numbers above. If any flagged input changes (market values, tax rates, cash amount, achievable rent), the ranking can change — re-run the calculator on the Taxes page.
