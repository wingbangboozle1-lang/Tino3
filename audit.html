Privacy, source and audit limits

The public edition is intentionally a general framework. This page records its evidence standard, current-tree scope and limits without reproducing private findings.

What this edition contains

  • One clearly labelled public edition with the inherited Property A / already-owned Property B distinction.
  • Source-linked federal and California rules, an inheritance checklist, blank cost fields, questions, flagged unknowns and general stress cases.
  • No property-specific inputs, assessments, rent, utility use, insurance quote, owner-derived projections or individualized recommendation.
  • A static, no-input site: no form, script, analytics, tracking pixel or third-party asset is included in the public pages.

Verification standard

General legal and historical claims link directly to the official publisher. The source library states what each source supports, when it was checked and its limits. Generic public sources cannot verify a property's title, value, tax bill, rent, utility use, coverage or condition; those fields remain blank. Dollar thresholds shown are general rules, not owner-derived amounts. Rounding is not treated as anonymization, so property-specific amounts are omitted.

The economic examples are historical U.S. index or aggregate episodes, not property forecasts. Local law, current policy terms, utility tariffs, property condition and future tax law require a private, date-specific review.

Current tree versus history

This refresh replaces owner-derived material in the current public edition and retires non-allowlisted routes with content-free notices. It does not rewrite old commits, pull-request diffs, caches, forks, downloads or copies outside this repository. No claim of complete historical erasure is made.

The Pages workflow packages an explicit allowlist plus generic withdrawal notices. The deployment source and live artifact must be confirmed after merge; a successful local package check alone is not a deployment check.

Reproducible local checks

python3 scripts/build.py
python3 scripts/verify.py
python3 scripts/package_site.py
python3 scripts/privacy_boundary.py

These checks validate the current files and generated artifact; they cannot certify that old external copies were erased or that a private document is accurate.
