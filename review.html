Flagged for review and repository audit

Unverified figures, modelling caveats and the repository audit.

What this page is for

Three lists. The first is every figure on this site that is not verified against an official source, and where the record for it is actually held. The second is every modelling shortcut that materially affects the answer. The third is the audit of the repository this site is built from. None of these block the recommendation, but each one is a reason to check before acting.

How the model works

Method, in one paragraph

A deterministic model, rebuilt from assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json by scripts/model.py. House prices grow at a stated nominal rate, the assessed value rolls forward under the published rules with an inflation cap, fixed charges grow with inflation, running costs are inflated at the same rate, and each path is valued at the end of a thirty year horizon after selling costs and tax. Every monetary result is rounded to the nearest $10 before it is printed. There is no randomness, so the same inputs always produce the same outputs.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.

  • California Legislative Information - Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2
  • California State Board of Equalization - Publication 29, Property Taxpayers' Guide
  • Internal Revenue Service - Gifts and inheritances - inherited property basis and sale
  • Internal Revenue Service - Topic no. 409, Capital gains and losses
