One year's tax bill rose about 18% and another fell about 6% while the assessed value moved 2% and 1%. A change in fixed charges would explain moves of that kind; the bills' line items would confirm or refute it.

If Property B passes in the same estate

The records supplied do not say who holds title. If the parents do, the building changes ownership at the same death and is reassessed. In a multi-unit building the family-home exclusion covers only the unit that was the parent's principal residence and becomes the child's. The table shows the building reassessed in full. If the current owner is already the heir, none of this applies.
