# Executive summary

## Decision status: open

This edition provides a general framework, not a personalized recommendation. It keeps **Property A, the inherited asset**, separate from **Property B, an asset already owned by the recipient**. No option is ranked because verified property records, local rules, current costs and the decision-maker's priorities are not part of the public edition.

## What the guide does

- Organizes evidence needed to compare use, rental, sale or deferral options.
- Separates ownership, basis, income, occupancy and expense records for A and B.
- Summarizes selected federal and California inheritance, tax, property-tax and rental rules with official links.
- Provides blank checklists and a method for testing vacancies, repairs, insurance changes, inflation, market declines and future law changes.
- Flags missing facts rather than inferring or publishing them.

## Privacy boundary

The public edition contains no address, property description, assessment, rent, utility use, insurance estimate, owner-derived projection, trust detail or household-specific amount. Rounding is not treated as anonymization. The static Pages artifact is built from an allowlist; legacy routes and other tracked paths are replaced in that artifact by generic withdrawal notices. No private input is collected by the site.

This changes only the current publication. It does not erase or certify removal of earlier commits, pull-request diffs, caches, forks, downloads or copies outside the repository.

## Important limitations

The official source library describes the general rules checked for the November 12, 2026 edition and states the limits of each source. The library cannot establish a specific property's title, basis, eligibility, cost, rent, coverage, local jurisdiction or tax result. Statutes, indexed amounts, bill status and policies change; confirm the controlling rules and records with the appropriate agency and qualified professional before acting.

The historical economic episodes are examples of U.S. aggregate or market conditions, not forecasts for a particular property or investment. This guide is not legal, tax, appraisal or personalized financial advice.
