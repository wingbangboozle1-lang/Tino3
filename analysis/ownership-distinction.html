3. The [PROPERTY_B] Unit 3 Rent-Control Trap When Moving Into [PROPERTY_A]

[PROPERTY_B] has 3 livable units: 2 rented for $7,150/month ($85,800/year) and 1 unrented unit available for [BENEFICIARY] to live in without losing rental income. Under CA R&TC § 218 and § 63.2, [BENEFICIARY] can claim only one principal residence. Moving out of [PROPERTY_B] Unit 3 into [PROPERTY_A] creates a costly dilemma:

  • Sub-Option L1 (Move into [PROPERTY_A] & rent out [PROPERTY_B] Unit 3): Generates initial market rent on Unit 3 (~$3,580/month in 2026 dollars), but if [PROPERTY_B] was built on or before June 13, 1979 (City B Rent Board), renting Unit 3 permanently subjects it to annual rent caps of 60% of CPI and strict just-cause eviction rules — yet still finishes $2,125,110 behind selling [PROPERTY_A].
  • Sub-Option L2 (Move into [PROPERTY_A] & keep [PROPERTY_B] Unit 3 unrented): Preserves Unit 3 free of tenant eviction restrictions, but costs an additional $650,060 over 10 years — finishing $2,775,170 behind selling [PROPERTY_A].
