How Much Money Can Be Inherited Alongside [PROPERTY_A] Without Incurring Estate or Inheritance Tax?

At the Local & California State Level: Unlimited ($0 tax). Neither California nor County A / City B imposes any estate, inheritance, or death transfer tax (CA R&TC § 13301; CA Controller; CA R&TC § 11930), and inherited cash is not taxable income (FTB) — except pre-tax Traditional IRAs/401(k)s, which are taxed as ordinary income when distributed over 10 years (IRS).

At the Federal Level: In 2026, a single decedent transfers up to $15,000,000 tax-free ($15,480,000 in 2027 per IR-2026-133; double with spousal portability on Form 706), with a 40% tax (26 U.S.C. § 2001) on excess value. Because [PROPERTY_B] is already owned by [BENEFICIARY], ONLY [PROPERTY_A] counts against [GRANTOR]'s threshold — leaving $11,900,000 of tax-free cash headroom in 2026 and $17,612,280 (single) / $41,755,800 (married couple with portability) in the 2045 baseline!
