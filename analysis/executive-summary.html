Four High-Priority Pre-Inheritance Checks for [GRANTOR] & [BENEFICIARY]

Check 1 · Trust Type
IRC § 1014

Confirm the trust holding [PROPERTY_A] is a revocable living trust included in [GRANTOR]'s gross estate. Under IRS Rev. Rul. 2023-2, an excluded irrevocable trust loses the basis step-up (a ~$3,216,390 tax hit in BS-8).

Check 2 · Spousal Portability
Form 706

If [GRANTOR] is a married couple, file IRS Form 706 within 9 months (+6-mo Form 4768 extension) of the first spouse's death to lock in DSUE portability — doubling estate-tax-free capacity to $48,287,030 in 2045.

Check 3 · Solar NEM Date
20-Yr Sunset

Record [PROPERTY_A]'s exact utility interconnection date. Under CPUC D.14-03-041, retail NEM credits expire 20 years after interconnection (by or before 2046), shifting electricity from a ~$180/yr credit to a net cost unless paired with battery storage.

Check 4 · Crash Playbook
60/40 or DCA

Avoid deploying 100% of [PROPERTY_A] sale proceeds into equities on Day 1 at peak valuations. Holding 40% in CA-tax-exempt U.S. Treasuries (31 U.S.C. § 3124) protects against a 2008-style sequence-of-returns crash (BS-3).
