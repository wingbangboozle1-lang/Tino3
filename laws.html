What the law says now, and what may change

Enacted, dead and proposed rules that could move the answer.

Read the election results carefully

The November 2026 California results on this page are unofficial, published by the Secretary of State with all 23,860 precincts partially reporting as of 14 November 2026, 5:16 p.m. Vote-by-mail, provisional and other ballots continue to be processed and counted during the canvass, and the results are not certified until 11 December 2026. They are included because Proposition 3 directly changes the tax on a property sale. Confirm against the certified results before relying on any of them.

The measure that would have mattered most did not reach the ballot. The initiative to repeal most of Proposition 19's inheritance changes failed to qualify, so the shelter arithmetic on this site is current law rather than a law about to change. The failure memo and the Secretary of State's live qualification dashboard are both linked below; the dashboard is the page to re-read every year at review gate RG-3, because a replacement measure can be filed at any time.

How this page is kept current

The owner asked for the official federal, state and local authorities to be rechecked periodically and for enacted changes to be kept apart from proposals. The rule this page follows: an entry is enacted only when a statute, a chaptered bill or a certified election result supports it, proposed only while a live official filing supports it, and dead once the publisher records that it failed. Nothing is moved between those groups on the strength of a news report. The recheck happens at review gate RG-3 on the pre-commitment page: once a year, in the same month, re-open the links below and record whether anything moved.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.

  • California Legislative Information - Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2
  • California State Board of Equalization - Publication 29, Property Taxpayers' Guide
