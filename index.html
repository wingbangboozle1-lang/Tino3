The short answer. Inherited property takes a new tax basis equal to its value at death, so selling soon afterwards produces almost no income tax. Keeping Property A costs about 1.5% of its value every year in property tax, insurance and upkeep and earns nothing unless someone pays rent. In every case tested, selling and reinvesting ends with more money than living in the house or renting it out. Living in it is still a reasonable choice if the heir wants that home: the page Live, rent or sell shows what that choice costs.
