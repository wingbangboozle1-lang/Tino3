Recommendation

1. Do not decide now

Nothing in current law requires a decision before the death occurs. Pre-committing to a sale, a rental conversion or a move locks in values and rules that will be different.

2. Sell [PROPERTY_A] at inheritance

Inherited basis steps up to date-of-death value, so a prompt sale inside the first year realises almost no gain. That is the only moment the embedded gain disappears.

3. Keep [PROPERTY_B] rented

It already produces the housing the household needs through its third unit, so occupying A buys no housing benefit and costs $1,972,250 in terminal wealth at 2045.

The ranking is close: renting A beats selling if long-run returns fall below about 6.5%, or if a future full-exclusion proposal becomes law — the 2025–26 petition failed to qualify for the November 2026 ballot (rent then gains about $719,050). Both branches are shown in full on the decision page.
