Read the two threshold tables together

The federal exclusion is indexed to inflation and has been rising. The Proposition 19 shelter is adjusted only every second February and only by the change in the California house price index. If house prices rise faster than the shelter, the shelter buys a smaller and smaller share of the property each time the transfer is delayed. On the baseline assumptions the shelter covers about 29% of Property A's modelled 2045 value. Waiting is not neutral.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.
