Projections, 2026 to 2055

Every year, both houses, on stated assumptions. The assessed value follows statute; the market value follows an assumption; the running costs follow the owner's figures and published escalation.

Read this first

The assessed-value and property-tax columns are arithmetic on published rules and are reliable to the same degree as the 2025 reconciliation. The market-value column is an assumption — 3.0% a year — and nothing more. No projection of this length is a forecast, and the decision analysis is built so that the conclusion does not depend on it.
