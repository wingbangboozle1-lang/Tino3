EVIDENCE-FIRST PLANNING

50-year reference · 2026–2076

Real historical volatility replayed forward as a stress reference. The property-specific market values, assessments and tax bills are deliberately blank, not forecasts disguised as facts.

How to read this

Start with a purely illustrative $1,000,000 assessed-value unit in 2026, then apply the BOE's actual 1977–2026 annual assessment factors to 2027–2076. Reindex FHFA's statewide Q2 price index to 100.00 at 2026. This repeats history; it does not predict an address or its tax bill.

Decision-year checkpoints

The dollar columns show a normalized assessment unit at the constitutional 1% base levy, excluding bonds, charges, reassessment, new construction and Proposition 8 relief.

Why real bills will differ

A and B have different tax-rate areas and base years; a death-time change in ownership can reset A, while B is already owned. Official factors alone cannot recover a parcel's assessed value. During a price crash, Proposition 8 may temporarily reduce the enrolled value.
