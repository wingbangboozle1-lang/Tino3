Cost inflation behaviour

Costs are inflated at 2.5% and the assessed value at 2.0%. Over thirty years that divergence is what makes the property tax a shrinking share of value: [PROPERTY_A]'s tax is 0.24% of market value in 2026 and 0.14% in 2055. The same arithmetic applies after any reassessment, which is why a rental conversion is so expensive.
