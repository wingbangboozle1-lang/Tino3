Two irregularities in the bill column are on the Flagged for Review page: in two places the same tax amount is entered for consecutive years although the assessed value rose 2%. Reading the table: 2017 is the one year the bill fell while the assessment rose. 2018 repeats 2017's bill. 2019 and 2020 repeat each other in the same way. A change in the fixed charges on the bill, or a bill entered twice, would explain both; the line items would settle it (question Q-19).

What the inheritance does to the assessed value

If a child moves in, the new assessed value is the old one plus whatever market value exceeds the old value and the Proposition 19 amount together. With this home that is simply market value less the amount. If the home is rented, it is assessed at full market value. The amount grows by one year's house price change every second year.
