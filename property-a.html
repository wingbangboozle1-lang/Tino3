The single most important number on this page

The assessed value is about $550,540 and the market value is about $3,100,000. Proposition 13 protects that gap only until ownership changes. When the trust transfers the house, the assessed value resets to market value, less the Proposition 19 shelter of about $1,886,640 if the heir moves in within a year and files the claim, or with no shelter at all if not. Either way this is the largest single change in the whole analysis, and it happens by operation of law whether or not anyone plans for it.

Solar and the electricity credit

The owner reports that the solar export credit currently exceeds usage, producing a small net credit rather than a bill. Two cautions. First, net energy metering tariffs are closed to new enrolment and since 15 April 2023 new systems take service on the net billing tariff, where export compensation is usually below the retail rate. A customer already on a NEM tariff may stay on it for twenty years from interconnection. Second, an inherited or sold property can lose the tariff holder status that goes with the original customer. Whether this system is on NEM 2.0 or on the net billing tariff is on the questions page.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.
