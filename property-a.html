Three tax paths

Occupation, rent and sale can create very different reassessment and income-tax outcomes.

UseCurrent ruleVerified personal amountOfficial reference
If inherited and rentedGenerally reassess at the then market value; the family-home exclusion ordinarily does not apply to rental use.—California BOE ↗ · California Legislature ↗
If inherited and lived inEligible parent-child transfer may preserve part of the factored base year value, only when both principal-residence and filing conditions hold.—California Legislature ↗ · California BOE ↗
If sold soon after deathBasis may generally be death-date fair market value when §1014 applies. Gain, costs and estate-tax exposure still require a calculation.—IRS ↗ · IRS ↗
Current value limit is not a future estimate

The 2025–27 family-home add-on is about $1,044,590 after $10 rounding. The actual 2040–2050 limit, eligibility and tax-area levy cannot be confirmed in 2026.
