Property tax

This is the recurring cost that decides the case. It is set by two numbers nobody controls — the assessed value and the county's rate — and by one event you can plan for: the reassessment at death.

What happens to Property A at death

Because a rented or non-occupied house does not qualify for the Prop 19 parent-child exclusion, the assessor resets Property A's taxable value to its market value on the date of death. At a 2045 inheritance that is a jump from about $802,030 to about $5,435,870, and the annual bill moves from about $10,980 to $64,220. That difference — roughly $53,240 a year — is the price of keeping the house instead of selling it.

Proposition 19, precisely

The exclusion is narrow, capped, and deadline-driven. Most inherited rentals get nothing.

QuestionAnswerSource
Who can use the parent-child exclusionOnly for a principal residence transferred from a parent to a child (or grandparent to grandchild where the parents are deceased), and the child must also use it as a principal residence.BOE Prop 19↗
How much value is protectedThe transferor's taxable value plus an adjusted $1,000,000. For transfers from 16 Feb 2025 to 15 Feb 2027 that adjusted amount is $1,044,586.BOE NR 25-02↗
What happens above the capThe excess is added to the taxable value. New taxable value = market value − $1,044,586 (in the current adjustment period).BOE Prop 19↗
What about a second home or a rentalThe exclusion for other real property was eliminated by Prop 19. A rented house is fully reassessed to market value at death.BOE Prop 19↗
DeadlinesHomeowners' or disabled veterans' exemption within 1 year; the exclusion claim within 3 years or before a transfer to a third party.BOE Prop 19↗
Change in ownership statementThe trustee must file it within 150 days after the date of death. Penalty for not filing on request is the greater of $100 or 10% of the new tax, capped at $5,000.RTC 480↗
When the reassessment takes effectAs of the date of death, not the date the property is distributed to you.SCC Assessor 2026↗

The $1,044,586 figure is the current adjustment period. The BOE re-indexes it every two years; the amount that will apply in 2040-2050 is not published and is modelled as inflating at 3.0% (F-02).

Other mechanics to expect

MechanicWhat it means for youSource
Supplemental assessmentA separate bill for the difference between the old and new taxable value, prorated to the months left in the fiscal year. It is not usually escrowed or paid at closing.SCC Assessor 2026↗ SF Tax Collector↗
Escape assessmentIf the reassessment is late, the county can bill prior years. An installment plan over four years exists in San Francisco.SF Tax Collector↗
Appeal windowSanta Clara County: informal review 1 July to 1 August; formal appeal 2 July to 15 September. A supplemental or escape assessment has 60 days.SCC Assessor 2026↗
Decline in valueIf the market falls below the factored base, the assessor must reduce the value temporarily; the base still grows 2% a year underneath.SCC Assessor 2026↗
Homeowners' Exemption$7,000 of assessed value, worth about $70 to $80 a year, for an owner-occupied principal residence. Recording a deed or a trust transfer terminates it.SCC Assessor 2026↗

Budget the supplemental bill in the first year after death; it is the most commonly missed cash call.
