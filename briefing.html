Attorney and CPA briefing sheet

A one-page brief for the attorney and CPA. Print it from this page.

How to print this sheet

Use your browser's print command. This section is set to print on one page of letter-size paper, without the navigation.

Anonymised. Two California properties. Prepared from public sources and owner-supplied figures, rounded to the nearest $10. Not legal, tax or investment advice.

Question first

Is the heir a child of the transferor? The reassessment relief in section 63.2 applies only to a parent and child, or a grandparent and grandchild where the middle generation has died. If the answer is no, the saving on this site does not exist.

For the attorney

  1. Is the heir a child of the transferor under Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2, including the statutory definitions (A-1)?
  2. Is the house titled in the trust now, and what is the recorded chain (questions page, section A)?
  3. Does the trust allow the trustee to park sale proceeds in Treasuries and then buy stocks on a phased schedule?
  4. Which estate tax return and extension position applies, and what is the portability election decision?

For the CPA

  1. Does the Proposition 19 occupancy and claim timetable match the planned move or letting date (questions page, section C)?
  2. What is the income tax basis at the date of death, and how is the sale reported?
  3. How are the rent and property tax treated on the Property B return?
  4. Is the phased sale-and-purchase plan consistent with the state and federal income tax position?
  5. Which insurance and water costs are documented, rather than owner estimates?

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.

  • California Legislative Information - Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2
  • California State Board of Equalization - Publication 29, Property Taxpayers' Guide
  • Internal Revenue Service - Gifts and inheritances - inherited property basis and sale
  • Internal Revenue Service - Topic no. 409, Capital gains and losses
