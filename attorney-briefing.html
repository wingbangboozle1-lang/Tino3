Privacy and accuracy notice

No identifiers. No name, street address, parcel number, ZIP code, trust term, beneficiary detail or utility account appears on this sheet. The homes are called Property A (the single-family home being inherited through a trust, planned 2040 to 2050, in Santa Clara County) and Property B (the three-unit building already owned by the heir, in the City and County of San Francisco).

Owner figures are unverified. Values, bills, rent and cost estimates come from the owner's own records and are labelled unverified unless an official public record was checked. They must be confirmed against the county roll, the tax bills, a written appraisal and real quotes before any filing.

First gate: parent-and-child relationship (condition of everything)

The intergenerational exclusion in Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2 applies only where the transfer is between a parent and a child, or between a grandparent and a grandchild whose parent has died. If the relationship is different, there is no exclusion: Property A is reassessed to full market value on transfer, every shelter figure below falls away, and the case for selling gets stronger.

Action for the attorney: confirm the relationship and read the trust instrument and the recorded deed before relying on any Proposition 19 figure. California Legislative Information · California State Board of Equalization

Recommendation

Sell Property A soon after it is inherited, unless the heir genuinely wants to live in that house.

Selling converts a concentrated, illiquid asset into capital at almost no tax (basis resets to value at death), and removes a property tax bill that is projected to multiply from about $10,840 today to about $79,330 from 2045 if the home is let out, or $57,060 if the heir moves in and claims the exclusion.

Keeping the home beats selling only if it appreciates about 7.72% a year for thirty years. The verified house price index for the area compounded at 6.9% a year over fifty years and 3.3% a year over the last twenty.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed.
