What an insurance benchmark does and does not tell you

The California Department of Insurance publishes average written premiums from its Residential Property Insurance Report. The statewide average for an owner-occupied homeowners policy was about $1,070 in experience year 2017. Both properties here are carried at $1,500 a year, which is an owner estimate and is flagged unverified.

The benchmark is statewide and across all amounts of insurance. It is not a premium for either building, and it must not be read as one: a three-unit rental in the city and a single-family home in the county are priced on entirely different bases. It is published here only so the owner estimate can be seen against something official, which is the most this source can support. A property-specific premium remains blank until it is quoted.

Three cost numbers that need replacing

  1. Insurance on both properties. The California Department of Insurance does not publish an average premium by area, so no official figure exists to check these against. Get three quotes for each building, one of which should be a rental policy. See suggestion S-01.
  2. Maintenance. No figure was supplied. This site assumes 0.5% of value a year for an owner-occupied home, 0.75% for Property A as a rental and 1.5% for the older three-unit building. Replace these with the actual five-year repair history.
  3. Water. The provider and tariff for Property A are not confirmed, so the owner's range is used as supplied.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.

  • California Legislative Information - Revenue and Taxation Code section 63.2
  • California State Board of Equalization - Publication 29, Property Taxpayers' Guide
  • Internal Revenue Service - Gifts and inheritances - inherited property basis and sale
  • Internal Revenue Service - Topic no. 409, Capital gains and losses
