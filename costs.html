Landlord / tenant law that affects SF rent

  • SF Rent Ordinance (Admin Code Ch. 37): rent increases capped for covered units; eviction only for 17 just causes (Sec.37.9(a)) - owner-occupancy is cause #8 with strict limits. sfgov.org
  • State rent control (units not covered by SF Ordinance): 5% + CPI or 10%, whichever lower. sfgov.org
  • Rental income is taxed as ordinary income (federal Schedule E; CA passive-activity rules, FTB Form 3801). Nonresident owners still pay CA tax on CA-source rent.
