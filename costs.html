Running costs

A blank evidence template for property tax, insurance, water, electricity/solar, maintenance, repairs, rental income and transaction costs.

Every property-specific amount is intentionally blank. A rounded public figure can still identify a property. Use actual records and quotes in a private worksheet; the source links below describe rules, not the cost of either property.

Keep cash flow and tax treatment separate

For a rental, organize actual cash received and paid by year. Then separately determine taxable rental income and any allowed deductions. IRS Publication 527 distinguishes, among other things, routine repairs from capital improvements and discusses rental expenses; it does not establish a property's budget.

For personal occupancy, include the changed housing arrangement and any rental income lost or reduced. For an inherited sale, keep basis and transaction-cost documents separate. Do not substitute a statewide average, insurance comparison survey or property-tax rule for a property-specific bill or quote.
