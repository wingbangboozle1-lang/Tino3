Laws, and what is being discussed

Everything below is separated into what is enacted, what is scheduled, and what is merely proposed. A proposal is not a rule and is not priced into any projection on this site.

How to read the status column

ENACTED and IN FORCE rows are law today and are priced into the model. SCHEDULED rows are law but the amount is not yet published — those are flagged. PROPOSED and UNKNOWN rows are not priced in. No claim is made here about the current status of any pending bill beyond what the cited official page states.

Three things that would change the answer

  1. A California wealth or holding tax. It would be a recurring cost on ownership and would strengthen the case for selling, because the sale converts a taxable holding into a smaller, movable one.
  2. Any weakening of the step-up in basis. The sell-early conclusion depends on a sale near the date-of-death value producing no gain. If basis step-up were repealed, an immediate sale would carry a large tax and the arithmetic would change substantially.
  3. A sharp rise in long-run appreciation expectations. Renting overtakes selling at about 5.4% a year, which is above the 2026 one-year reading but not far above the long-run California trend. This is the most plausible way the recommendation flips.
