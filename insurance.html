How to replace the blanks

  1. Open the state comparison tool, choose the county and city, the type of cover, the age of the home and the coverage amount, and note the range of premiums.
  2. Ask a licensed broker for three quotes for Property A as an owner-occupied home, with replacement cost matched to rebuilding cost rather than market value.
  3. Ask for three landlord quotes for Property B as a three-unit building, with liability and loss-of-rent cover.
  4. Ask each insurer about wildfire surcharges and price earthquake cover separately.
  5. Requote when the property changes hands: a policy in a parent's or trust's name does not automatically continue for an heir, and a change from owner-occupied to rented needs a different policy.
