Methodology

Every modelled number on this site can be re-derived from this page. Official facts are linked; assumptions are labelled and changeable on the Decision Analysis page.

Rounding

All dollars rounded to the nearest $10 (per brief). Percentages shown to 2 decimals. Blank = no official source — never inferred.

What is NOT modelled

Income tax on rental income (depends on your bracket), mortgage (none stated), vacancy, major repairs, and law changes beyond the watch list. The Decision Analysis shows before-income-tax nets so you can apply your own bracket.
