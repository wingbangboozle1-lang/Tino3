Inheritance Process — Step by Step (via Living Trust)

General information from official sources, not legal advice. Statute text: California Legislative Information. Prop 19 filings: BOE. Federal forms: IRS.

Phase 1 — Before death (do now)

1
Fund the trust. Record deeds transferring both properties into the trust name; retitle accounts. Unfunded trust assets fall back to probate (CA Probate Code, leginfo).
2
Decide occupancy intent per property. Prop 19 exclusion requires the child to occupy the family home as a primary residence within 1 year. Rentals get no exclusion (BOE). Document the intent in the trust.
3
Preserve step-up. Hold appreciated property until death (IRC §1014); avoid lifetime gifts of appreciated property (carryover basis, IRC §1015). (IRS)
4
Calendar the deadlines. Homeowners' exemption within 1 year; BOE-19-P within 3 years (or before sale). Missing them forfeits the exclusion (BOE).

Phase 2 — At death (first 90 days)

5
Obtain death certificates; notify successor trustee. Trustee accepts appointment under trust terms; no probate court needed for funded trust assets.
6
Secure properties; notify insurers, utilities, tenants. Property B tenants keep their leases; SF Rent Ordinance protections continue (leginfo).
7
Inventory & appraise. Get date-of-death FMV appraisals for both properties (sets step-up basis, IRC §1014) and for Prop 19 reassessment. Keep appraisal reports — they are the basis evidence.
8
Determine federal filing. Form 706 required only if gross estate exceeds $15M (2026) or portability elected. Below threshold: no return, but keep appraisals. (IRS)

Phase 3 — Transfer & reassessment (months 1–12)

9
Distribute per trust terms. Record trustee's deed / affidavit of death of trustee with county recorder (Santa Clara County for A; SF for B).
10
File Prop 19 claims (Property A, if occupying). Homeowners' exemption (BOE-266) within 1 year + BOE-19-P within 3 years with the county assessor (BOE). Property B (rental): expect full reassessment — budget accordingly.
11
Update insurance & utilities. Owner-occupied (HO-3) vs. landlord (DP-3); transfer SJ Water / SFPUC / PG&E accounts. Under NBT, solar billing follows the meter, not the owner (CPUC).
12
Execute the chosen path. Move in (A), lease (A), or sell (A) — see Decision Analysis. If selling: step-up means gain ≈ post-death growth only; report on Form 8949 / Schedule D.

Phase 4 — Ongoing

13
Pay property tax on the new base. Installments due Nov 1 / Feb 1; 10% late penalty. Appeal via county Assessment Appeals Board if assessed above market (Prop 8).
14
Review annually. Exclusion indexed for inflation; Prop 19 cap adjusted biennially; rent/insurance markets move. Re-run the Decision Analysis with fresh quotes.
