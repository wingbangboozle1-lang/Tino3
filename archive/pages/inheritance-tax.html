OBBBA / Working Families Tax Cuts (P.L. 119-21, signed Jul 4, 2025): the $15M exclusion is permanent from 2026 and inflation-indexed thereafter — the scheduled TCJA sunset to ~$7M did not occur. Congress can still amend it in future. (IRS)

Worked example (modelled): estate of $4,900,000 (both houses at family-provided 2026 values) → federal estate tax $0 (below $15M). Estate of $16,000,000 → tax = 40% × ($16,000,000 − $15,000,000) = $400,000. Every $1,000,000 over the exclusion costs $400,000.
