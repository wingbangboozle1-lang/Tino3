Inheritance Process - Step by Step (Trust, California)

Assumes Property A/B are held in a revocable living trust (confirm with attorney). If they are not in trust, formal probate applies (9-18 months, courts.ca.gov) - see Step 2B. This is a checklist, not legal advice.

Before death (now - 2040s)

  1. Confirm both properties are titled in the trust (deed recorded with Santa Clara / SF County Recorder). Unfunded assets fall into probate.
  2. Confirm successor trustee and successor beneficiary roles; keep trust + deeds + solar/NEM paperwork + insurance policies in one binder.
  3. Get property-specific insurance quotes (HO-3 for Cupertino; DP-3 landlord for SF) and save them - benchmarks on this site are not quotes.
  4. Obtain Cupertino comparable-rent quotes (fills the blank rent cell) and SF lease copies.
  5. Review Prop 19 deadlines with the estate attorney now - the 1-year move-in clock cannot be extended after death.

At death - trust path (Step 2A)

  1. Obtain death certificates (multiple certified copies).
  2. Trustee accepts / successor trustee steps in. Within 60 days of death (or of becoming trustee, whichever later), give written notice to all beneficiaries and heirs (Prob. Code Sec.16061.7).
  3. Within 150 days, give written notice to the County Assessor for each county where trust real property sits (Santa Clara + SF).
  4. Inventory & value assets as of date of death - this appraisal is the step-up basis (IRC 1014). Keep it permanently.
  5. File final income-tax returns for [DECEDENT] (federal Form 1040 + CA Form 540); file estate/trust returns (Form 1041 / CA Form 541) if the estate/trust earns income. File Form 706 only if the gross estate exceeds the exemption ($15M/person in 2026, indexed).
  6. Pay debts, taxes, and administration expenses from the estate/trust; notify creditors as required.
  7. Distribute per trust terms; record deeds / transfer title to [BENEFICIARY] or to the continuing trust.

At death - probate path, only if assets are NOT in trust (Step 2B)

  1. File Petition for Probate (Form DE-111) in the county of [DECEDENT] residence; filing fee typically $435 (courts.ca.gov).
  2. Give notice to heirs/devisees + publish notice in a newspaper; court hearing appoints personal representative.
  3. Inventory & Appraisal (DE-160) with probate referee; Notice to Creditors (DE-157); pay debts/taxes.
  4. Account to the court (~1 year), then petition for final distribution; hearing; distribute. Total typically 9-18 months.

After transfer - execute the chosen option

  • Live in: update insurance to HO-3 primary residence, transfer solar/NEM account, file exemption (above).
  • Rent: landlord policy (DP-3), leases compliant with SF Rent Ordinance / CA tenant law, Schedule E tax reporting.
  • Sell: list promptly to preserve step-up benefit; report sale on Schedule D; reinvest net proceeds per investment policy.
