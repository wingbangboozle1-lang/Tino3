Transfer workflow

This is a general checklist, not confirmation of the terms of any private trust. An attorney and fiduciary must determine the actual authority, beneficiaries, deadlines and tax treatment.

StepWhenAction / controlOfficial source / scope
01 · Establish authorityBefore distributing or sellingPrivately review trust, recorded title, ownership fractions, successor trustee and whether property is included in the taxable estate. A trust label alone is not sufficient.IRS · Estate tax ↗BOE · August 2026 guidance ↗
02 · Secure records and cashImmediately after death · planning actionObtain needed records, preserve insurance and reserve liquidity; inventory cash, securities, real estate, insurance and other includible assets without publishing them.IRS · Estate tax ↗CDI · Finding residential insurance ↗
03 · Trustee notificationsGenerally within 60 days of triggering eventServe required beneficiaries/heirs; vacancy and later-known-person exceptions affect timing. Counsel should verify the statutory notice and contest provisions.California · Probate Code 16061.7 ↗
04 · Date-of-death valuationAt transfer valuation dateObtain supportable fair market value and basis records; coordinate any alternate valuation election and basis consistency reporting.IRS · Inherited property basis ↗IRS · Form 706 instructions ↗
05 · Ownership statementGenerally within 150 days after death for nonprobate transfersTrustee/transferee files in each relevant county. Probate cases use the inventory/appraisal filing timeline specified by statute.California · Revenue & Taxation Code 480 ↗
06 · Residence choiceWithin one year of transfer for exemption filingIf eligible and actually occupying the family home, apply for homeowners or disabled veterans exemption and submit Proposition 19 claim; claim generally within 3 years or before third-party transfer. Do not wait for trust administration to finish.BOE · Proposition 19 ↗
07 · Federal return and liquidityCheck death-year Form 706 deadlineEvaluate filing, available exclusion, prior gifts, spouse election, deductions and estate tax. Do not distribute funds needed for obligations.IRS · Form 706 instructions ↗IRS · Estate tax ↗
08 · Operating planAfter verifying occupancy and tax positionGet actual policy quotes, verify leases and units, allocate mixed-use costs, then calculate hold vs rent vs sell after tax.IRS · Rental property ↗CDI · Finding residential insurance ↗SF · Rental inventory compliance ↗
09 · Distribute and monitorAs authorized by trust / advisersMaintain basis and assessment records, account for income from inherited assets and revisit exclusion when occupancy changes. This checklist does not determine a distribution date.IRS · Inherited property basis ↗BOE · August 2026 guidance ↗FTB · Gifts and inheritance ↗
