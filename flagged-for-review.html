Flagged for review

Everything on this site that is not verified against an official source, is an assumption, or is simply missing. Nothing here has been quietly filled in.

The rule applied

A value that could not be confirmed from an official source was left blank and listed here. Where a computation needed a number that does not exist, the assumption is printed next to the result rather than buried. Four items carry High impact: the estate's cash (F-01), the market rent for Property A (F-06), insurance (F-10), and whether any of the inheritance is pre-tax retirement money (F-14).
