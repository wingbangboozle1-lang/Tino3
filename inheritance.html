Inheriting through a trust, step by step

The trust transfer, phase by phase, with deadlines.

How to read this

This is the procedural path, in order, with the rule that governs each step. It is deliberately generic: it does not restate the trust, which this site has not seen. Where a deadline is set by statute the statute is linked so the current text can be read.

The two deadlines that actually bite

  1. Move in within one year if the Proposition 19 exclusion is going to be claimed. The guidance is explicit that there are no exceptions to that rule, and that the exclusion is removed when the home stops being the principal residence.
  2. File the exclusion claim within three years, or before transferring the property to anyone else, whichever is earlier.

Everything else on this list is administrative. These two are the ones that change the money.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.
