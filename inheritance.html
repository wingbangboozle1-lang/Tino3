Trust process

The steps after a parent's death when property is held in a living trust, in order, with the deadline for each.

StepWhenWhat to doStatusOfficial source
1NowConfirm the trust type, that each deed is titled in the trust and which home carries the Homeowners' Exemption. Find the solar connection date.Verified F-10CRT-SCC IRS-RR232
2At the deathThe successor trustee takes over. Assets properly held in the trust pass without probate; the trustee needs the trust document and death certificates.VerifiedCRT-SELF CRT-SCC
3Within 60 daysThe trustee serves the required notification on every beneficiary and heir once the trust becomes irrevocable.VerifiedCA-PROB16061
4First weeksGet an employer identification number for the trust and written date-of-death appraisals of both properties. The appraisals set the new tax basis.VerifiedIRS-1041 IRS-INHERIT
5Within 150 daysFile a change in ownership statement with the assessor in each county. Late filing after a written request brings a penalty.VerifiedCA-RTC480
6Within 1 yearIf keeping Property A as a home: move in and file the Homeowners' Exemption claim. A late claim gives relief only from the year it is filed.VerifiedBOE-P19 BOE-LTA2626
7Within 3 years, and before any saleFile the claim for the parent-to-child exclusion for Property A.VerifiedBOE-P19 BOE-LTA2626
8Within 9 monthsIf the estate exceeds the exclusion, or a spouse survives and wants the unused exclusion, file the federal estate tax return and pay any tax. A six-month filing extension is available.VerifiedIRS-706
9After the returnWhere an estate tax return is required, beneficiaries receive a statement of the value to use as basis.VerifiedIRS-706 IRS-P551
10Each tax yearFile the trust's federal income tax return if it has any taxable income or gross income of $600 or more, and the California return where required.Verified F-17IRS-1041 FTB-541
11Months after the deathExpect supplemental property tax bills for the reassessment. Unpaid instalments carry a 10% penalty.VerifiedSF-TAX
12When selling Property AReport the sale with the date-of-death value as basis. County transfer tax is $0.55 per $500 of value.VerifiedIRS-INHERIT SCC-DTT
13If letting any unitReport rent and expenses each year and keep depreciation records.VerifiedIRS-T414
14At the endThe trustee pays debts and distributes or continues to manage the assets as the trust directs.VerifiedCRT-SCC
Two clocks start at the date of death. The one-year move-in period for the family-home exclusion, and the nine-month estate tax deadline. Neither waits for the trust to be distributed.

Reviewed 2026-11-10. General information for planning, not legal, tax or investment advice. Confirm every step with a California estate attorney and a tax adviser before acting.
