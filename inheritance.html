What a trust does and does not do

  • A revocable trust keeps the property in the taxable estate, which is what resets the basis.
  • A trust does not prevent reassessment. The assessor looks through the trust to the people who benefit.
  • A revocable trust does not take property out of the federal taxable estate. The exclusion is what shelters it.
