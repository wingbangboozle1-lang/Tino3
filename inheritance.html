What the assessor will do

On a nonexcluded change of ownership the assessor resets taxable value to market value and issues a supplemental assessment for the part-year. The Proposition 19 formula caps the increase only where the family-home conditions are met: the assessed value becomes the factored base year value plus any excess over base year value plus the adjusted cap. Documents to assemble now, not at death

  • Trust instrument and any amendments, plus the schedule of trust assets.
  • Current deeds and the county assessor's latest value notice for each property.
  • The last two secured property-tax bills for each property (this edition could not verify either 2026 bill).
  • Insurance declarations pages and the last two utility bills.
  • Leases and rent ledgers for [PROPERTY_B]'s rented units.
  • A written record of improvements and capital expenditure for basis and depreciation support.
