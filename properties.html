The one input that would change the most

Which of the two houses is the transferor's principal residence. If it is Property B and you occupy it after the death, Proposition 19 can hold its taxable value near the transferor's base plus the adjusted cap instead of resetting to market. On today's numbers that is roughly $641,170 of taxable value, or about $7,580 a year of property tax at the San Francisco rate. It is not a certainty, it requires occupancy, and it requires filing on time. See property tax.
