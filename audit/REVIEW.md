# Independent privacy and source audit

**Status: needs review; not a certification.** Reviewed against the main-branch dashboard at merge commit `3f32918` on 2026-11-10. This file does not reproduce private information. The site owner should publish a privacy-safe version of these findings on its Flagged for Review page.

## Urgent privacy and integration

- The repository is public, GitHub Pages is enabled, and the existing site includes linked household-specific property, financial, rental, and utility details. Removing names or rounding figures does not make the combination anonymous. The same material is in tracked JavaScript and public Git history, as well as HTML. Remove private inputs and any derived individualized outputs from **all** public assets; deletion from the latest commit alone will not erase public history, caches, clones, prior PRs, or already accessed copies. Coordinate history-remediation options with the repository owner; never claim past exposure has been erased. See [GitHub's sensitive-data removal guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/keeping-your-account-and-data-secure/removing-sensitive-data-from-a-repository).
- Other active session branches have overlapping file claims. The published site is owned by another session. This audit requests remediation rather than editing its claimed files. Issue [#4](https://github.com/wingbangboozle1-lang/Tino3/issues/4) and PR [#2](https://github.com/wingbangboozle1-lang/Tino3/pull/2) record the handoff without reproducing the details.
- User-supplied entries and derived individualized projections are not official-source-verified. They must not be shown as verified, and the user specifically requested blanks when no official confirmation exists. With privacy as the top priority, blanks/placeholders, not published user numbers, are appropriate. Public forecasts cannot be called verified facts.

## Source-checked issues

| Area | Finding and required treatment | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Family-home exclusion | Both transferor and transferee must meet the family-home requirements; relationship, transfer timing, occupancy, exemption claims, deadlines, and the transfer-date cap all matter. The adjusted assessed value is not always simply market value minus the cap: when applicable, it is the greater of the factored base-year value and market value minus the applicable exclusion amount. A calculator must not silently omit the base-year value or label modeled parcel amounts verified. | [California BOE Proposition 19](https://www.boe.ca.gov/prop19/) |
| Transfer-date cap | For transfers February 16, 2025 through February 15, 2027 the adjusted amount is $1,044,586 (display rounded to $1,044,590). The actual future amount is unknown, not a verified forecast. Use the unrounded amount internally. | [BOE Letter to Assessors 2025/009](https://www.boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/pdf/lta25009.pdf) |
| Estate tax | The 2026 basic exclusion is $15,000,000; the IRS has already announced $15,480,000 for 2027. Neither is a beneficiary-specific tax-free inheritance allowance. Gross estate, lifetime adjusted taxable gifts, deductions, portability elections, citizenship and other facts matter. A risk conclusion cannot be reached from property values alone. | [IRS 2027 inflation release](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2027-taxpayers-continue-to-benefit-from-the-working-families-tax-cuts), [IRS Form 706 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i706) |
| Basis | A trust does not automatically ensure a basis adjustment on death. For the facts in IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-2, assets of an irrevocable grantor trust excluded from the grantor's estate did not receive an adjustment. Do not claim every inheritance or trust asset automatically steps up. | [IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-2](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2023-16_IRB#REV-RUL-2023-2) |
| California death tax | Historical Franchise Tax Board analysis describes a zero pick-up tax because the federal state-death-tax credit was repealed for post-2004 deaths. It is historical analysis, not proof that all possible tax situations are tax-free; check current law and applicable estate circumstances. | [FTB analysis of SB 726](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/tax-pros/law/legislation/2017-2018/sb726-032317.pdf) |
| Maintenance | A Fannie Mae guide describes a 1%–4% rule of thumb. That does not verify a particular home’s actual costs or prove a different percentage is correct. Reserve estimates should be explicitly hypothetical and separate from verified costs. | [Fannie Mae maintenance guide](https://yourhome.fanniemae.com/own/how-build-your-maintenance-and-repair-budget) |
| San Francisco rent rules | Applicability of rent stabilization, eviction protections and exemptions depends on the dwelling and tenancy; the annual per-unit Rent Board fee also has distinct exemptions. Unit count alone does not establish the legal outcome. | [San Francisco Rent Board fee](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-fee-link), [SF ordinance 248-24](https://sfbos.archive.sf.gov/sites/default/files/o0248-24.pdf) |
| Pending law | BOE analyzed Senate Bill 284 in 2025 as a *proposal* regarding intergenerational transfers and probate timing. Its current status and actual enacted text must be checked before it is described as current law. | [BOE bill analysis](https://boe.ca.gov/legdiv/pdf/0284sb050725rd.pdf), [California legislative bill information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/) |
