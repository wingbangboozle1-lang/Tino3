Verification notes & irregularities

  • BOE URL: The old URL boe.ca.gov/proptaxes/prop-19.htm returns 404. Correct URL is boe.ca.gov/prop19/ — used throughout this site.
  • SF effective rate anomaly: User-provided SF figures imply a 2.13% effective rate vs. Cupertino's 1.34%. Both share the 1.00% Prop 13 base, so the gap implies large parcel-specific levies or a data error. Must verify from the SF tax bill.
  • Insurance: No official parcel-specific premium exists without a quote. All insurance figures are secondary averages — flagged accordingly. Do not treat as quotes.
  • Future law: Prop 19 caps, exemption indexing, and step-up rules can change. Future amounts are flagged unverified; only enacted law is shown as verified.
  • Rounding: All dollars rounded to nearest $10 per requirements. Percentages shown to 2 decimals.
