What this ledger does not do

  • It does not reproduce the rate schedules, thresholds or forms. Those change; read the linked document.
  • It does not cover local ordinances beyond the two cities named, and it does not verify which rules apply to which building.
  • It contains no paid, paywalled or secondary source. Everything here is an official publisher or an official government site.
  • It was checked on one date. Statutory sections are amended; letters to assessors are superseded; election results are certified after the fact.

How the numbers on this page are produced

Every computed figure on this page comes from scripts/model.py, which reads assets/data/inputs.json and assets/data/series.json. Monetary results are rounded to the nearest $10 before they are printed. The rules the model implements are the published official rules below; where a page uses a stated assumption or an unverified owner figure, that row says so and is logged on the register of unverified and assumed items.
