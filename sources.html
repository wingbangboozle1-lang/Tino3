Owner-supplied figures

These come from you and are not verified against a public record: both market values, both roll histories, both tax bills, the rent roll, the utility ranges and both insurance estimates. They are used as given, rounded to the nearest $10 for display, and are marked UNVERIFIED wherever they appear.

Computed by this project

Everything in the model that is arithmetic rather than published: the residual flat charges, the assessed-value and tax projections, the running-cost projections, the after-tax decision comparison, the break-even rent and the attribution of the estate headroom. The code is scripts/model.py and the outputs are the CSV files in data/. Every assumption used is printed on the projections page.

How verification was done

  • Each official page was opened and read for the specific sentence or table relied on, on 10 November 2026.
  • Where a page published a worked example (Santa Clara's $1,000,000 illustration), the model's formula was checked against it.
  • Both owner-supplied 2025 bills were reproduced from the official rate and the assessed value to within a few dollars before any forward projection was run.
  • Where an official page did not publish a number, the number was left blank and flagged — never filled in from a listing site, a news article or a comparable sale.
  • Two secondary sources are used only to corroborate, and are named as such: the FTB bill analyses for the wealth-tax discussion, and the FHFA series served through the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

Scope limit

This is a documented review of the specific rules the decision turns on. It is not a legal opinion, and it is not a line-by-line verification of the Revenue and Taxation Code. Statutes and county rates change; re-run the check before relying on any figure in 2040.
