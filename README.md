# Inheritance Atlas

An official-source, privacy-first inherited-property planning dashboard.

**Live site:** https://wingbangboozle1-lang.github.io/Tino3/

## Executive summary

- Preserve flexibility until trust terms, transfer-date valuation, occupancy eligibility and actual costs are established.
- Living in the inherited family home may qualify for Proposition 19 relief, but the exclusion is conditional and value-limited. Renting generally requires a reassessment analysis.
- Selling generally uses inherited date-of-death basis (subject to exceptions), not the original purchase price. Compare after-tax net proceeds with after-tax rental cash flow and housing costs across both properties.
- The 2026 federal estate filing threshold framework is $15,000,000; this is not a separate tax-free allowance per beneficiary. California's Controller says no California estate return is required for deaths on or after January 1, 2005.
- No supported profit ranking, property-specific insurance premium, future value or estate headroom is available. Requested unknown figures stay blank rather than being fabricated.

Official references and claim-specific scope appear on the site's Source library and in every data-table row. Dollars are displayed to the nearest $10; exact official thresholds govern filing.

## Privacy incident and remediation

Earlier parallel editions published financial data and household-derived scenarios. This update replaces their current HTML, datasets and generators with content-free withdrawal notices. This applies to the tracked branch-root files as well as the Pages artifact, so the legacy publisher cannot republish those versions from the current tree. Historical Git objects, prior PR diffs, cached pages and third-party copies are **not erased**. Do not describe this as a complete historical privacy cleanup.

The GitHub token cannot change the Pages publishing source (HTTP 403). Pages is configured for legacy publication from `main` root, alongside the repository's Actions deployment. Both now receive sanitized content. This is a mitigation of the dual-publisher privacy risk, not a claim that the configuration race has been eliminated.

No private evidence should be attached to public issues, PRs or forms. The reviewed dashboard has no scripts, forms, analytics, remote assets or browser storage. Property A and Property B are placeholders.

Verification covers eight reproducible pages, 119 cited table rows, approved source domains, local links, navigation, absence of forms/remote assets, and a fail-closed publication boundary for **all tracked non-allowlisted files**. Legacy generators are disabled to prevent accidental republication.

These checks do not establish exhaustive legal accuracy, external-link availability, browser rendering, insurer quotes or future economic facts. The retained evidence ledger records earlier review scope. This session rechecked IRS estate-tax and inherited-basis pages, BOE Proposition 19 and August 2026 guidance, the California Controller, SF Treasurer, CDI pricing limitations and the LAO proposal analysis. Form 706's opening contents were retrieved, not its entire 50-chunk instruction set. Proposed initiative outcome remains unverified, not enacted law in this model.

Content is a November 10, 2026 snapshot, not an automatic legal or market feed. Flagged findings do not block publication. Property-specific projections and a definitive maximum-profit recommendation remain incomplete under the no-inference requirement.
