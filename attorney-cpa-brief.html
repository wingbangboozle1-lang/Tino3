Questions for the attorney

  1. Is the transfer between a parent and a child, or a qualifying grandchild? No exclusion applies if not. Section 63.2
  2. Will the trust hold the home directly, or through an entity? Check the legal-entity rules. LTA 2026/026
  3. Who lives in the home, and can the homeowners' exemption be filed within one year? LTA 2026/026
  4. When must the notice to heirs be sent, once a revocable trust becomes irrevocable? Probate Code 16061.7
  5. Is a federal return required, given the estate's size and the year of death? IRS estate tax

Questions for the CPA

  1. Confirm the stepped-up basis at date of death and the value to be used. IRS basis rule
  2. Does the section 121 exclusion apply to any sale of Property A, and on what dates? IRS Pub 523
  3. What is the recapture and capital gain rate on rental property (Property B)? IRS tax topic 409
  4. Which California tax applies to rent and to gains after the inheritance? FTB inheritance
  5. Which solar tariff applies to the system (NEM 2.0 or net billing)? CPUC net metering

Deadlines

  • Within 9 months of death: Form 706 (if required) and any estate tax; extension by Form 4768 for filing only. IRS Form 706 instructions
  • Within 1 year of transfer: principal residence and homeowners' exemption (if the home is to be lived in). BOE LTA 2026/026
  • Within 3 years of transfer: intergenerational transfer claim. BOE Proposition 19
  • 16 February 2027: value cap amount changes. BOE LTA 2025/009
