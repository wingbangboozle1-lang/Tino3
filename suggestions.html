Suggestions for review

Proposed improvements are kept separate from the public edition; no suggestion is selected or implemented automatically.

Selection status: none recorded. This page is a review register, not a feature request form. Nothing listed below changes the site until it is separately reviewed and selected.

Proposals

Suggestions kept separate for review
IDProposalPrivacy / decision benefitStatus
S-01Keep all property-specific inputs in an offline, access-controlled workbook.Avoids publishing or collecting figures; allows private records and supporting documents.Proposed — not selected or implemented.
S-02Maintain two ledgers: A as inherited, B as already owned, with separate title, basis, rent and operating-cost records.Preserves the ownership distinction and reduces accidental double-counting.Proposed — not selected or implemented.
S-03Have a qualified tax/legal professional review any future property-specific calculations before use.Checks title, local law, occupancy, basis, tax treatment and deadlines without publishing personal records.Proposed — not selected or implemented.
S-04Recheck official rules at the transfer date, before a sale or occupancy change, and after material law changes.Prevents stale federal, state and local assumptions from being treated as permanent.Proposed — not selected or implemented.
S-05If a calculator is later selected, make it local-only and show sources, blank unknowns and stress cases.Avoids transmitting private inputs; separates fact, assumption and computation.Proposed — not selected or implemented.

Selection gate

Before any proposal is implemented, decide whether its benefit is worth the privacy and maintenance cost, define the inputs it would require, identify its official sources, and test that no private values enter a public artifact. The suggestions page itself does not collect a selection.
