How to review a suggestion

  1. Read the proposal, its benefit and the private inputs it would require.
  2. Check whether it can be done without putting personal values, addresses or parcel records into a public page or artifact.
  3. Identify the official source that would support any new factual row before it is published.
  4. Decide whether to adopt, defer or reject; record the choice offline. The site does not collect a selection.
  5. If adopted, re-run python3 scripts/build.py, python3 scripts/verify.py and python3 scripts/privacy_boundary.py and confirm the artifact remains allowlisted.

Selection gate

Before any proposal is implemented, decide whether its benefit is worth the privacy and maintenance cost, define the inputs it would require, identify its official sources, and test that no private values enter a public artifact. A reviewer should record: selected or declined, owner, files affected, privacy test, source recheck date, rollback plan and acceptance test. The suggestions page itself does not collect a selection.

Privacy reminder. Do not paste deeds, tax bills, rent ledgers, account numbers, exact assessments or quotes into a public issue, pull request or website field. Keep them in the private workbook described in S-01.
