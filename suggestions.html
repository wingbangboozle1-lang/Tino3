How to use this page

  1. Mark each row Adopt, Reject, or Defer — outside the site; the site takes no input and stores nothing.
  2. Adopted items that supply missing evidence (SG-01 to SG-07) close the matching flag on the Flagged for Review page and re-run the model.
  3. Process items (SG-08 to SG-12) change no figure; they change how safely the existing figures are used.
