2. Tax headline

  • Federal: $15,000,000/person exemption in 2026 (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32), made permanent by OBBBA (P.L. 119-21 Sec.70106), indexed from 2027; 40% above. Annual gift exclusion $19,000/recipient.
  • California: No estate tax, no inheritance tax. But Prop 19 controls property-tax reassessment - the single biggest cost lever in this plan.
  • Step-up basis: Inherited house basis resets to date-of-death fair market value (IRC 1014) - prompt sale approx. $0 capital gain.
  • Later sale: Federal long-term gains 0%/15%/20% + 3.8% NIIT over thresholds; California taxes gains as ordinary income 1%-12.3% (+1% over $1M). Primary-residence exclusion $250,000/$500,000 (IRC 121) only if lived there 2 of 5 years.
