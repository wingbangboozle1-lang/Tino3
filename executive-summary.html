What the heir should know now

  • The move-in rule is strict. To keep part of Property A's low assessed value the heir must make it a principal residence within one year of the death, with no exceptions, and must stay. Moving out later triggers the full reassessed value, measured as of the date of death.
  • Property B is reassessed whatever happens. Its property tax rises about elevenfold while rents stay limited. Its surplus after inheritance is small; review its future separately.
  • The solar credit will not last. Net metering ends 20 years after the system was connected, which is before or during the inheritance window.
  • The trust type decides the income tax. The plan assumes the properties are in the taxable estate and receive a new basis at death. Confirm it.
  • Nothing proposed today changes the plan. The initiative to restore the old inheritance rules failed to qualify in May 2026. Bills to repeal the federal estate tax have not moved since February 2025.
