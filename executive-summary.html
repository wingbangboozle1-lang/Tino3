EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

One recommendation.
Five numbers that drive it.

Prepared 10 November 2026 for a trust inheritance expected between 2040 and 2050.

◈
Evidence standard. Every rule below links to the official publisher. Scenario assumptions are labelled scenario; they are not forecasts.

1. The decision

Sell [PROPERTY_A] within twelve months of the transfer, keep [PROPERTY_B] as the rental and as the household's residence, and reinvest the proceeds in a diversified portfolio. If the household genuinely wants to live in [PROPERTY_A] for a decade or more, that choice is affordable but it is a lifestyle purchase, not the highest-wealth path: at 2045 it costs about $1,972,250 of terminal after-tax net worth against selling.

2. Why the sale wins — and how narrowly

  • Basis reset. Inherited property takes a basis equal to date-of-death fair market value, so a prompt sale realises almost no taxable gain. Delay converts that free reset into taxable appreciation. IRS · Gifts & inheritances ↗
  • The Proposition 19 exclusion is capped, and the cap is small relative to this property. The exclusion is the factored base year value plus $1,044,590 for transfers up to 15 February 2027 (official figure $1,044,586, shown rounded), and it requires the transferee to occupy the home as a principal residence and file within deadlines. BOE · BOE adjusts the Proposition 19 $1 million interge… ↗BOE · Proposition 19 ↗
  • Housing is already solved. [PROPERTY_B] has three livable units and only two are rented, so the household can live there without giving up rent. Occupying A therefore adds no housing value.
  • Renting A out is taxable and thin. A rental transfer is reassessed at full market value, so first-year tax at 2045 would be about $87,250 against gross rent of roughly $195,940 under the 3.0% yield scenario. The net margin is small relative to capital at risk.
  • Timing. The 2050 comparison is effectively a tie ($17,183,690 versus $16,760,930 in 2045 terms), so the edge depends on returns and appreciation, not on law.

4. Inheritance tax: the direct answer

There is no California inheritance, estate, gift or succession tax; the state is constitutionally prohibited from imposing one. CA statute · Revenue & Taxation Code 13301 - Prohibition o… ↗ A beneficiary therefore receives assets free of California death tax at any amount. The federal constraint sits on the estate, not on each beneficiary: a Form 706 is required when the gross estate plus adjusted taxable gifts exceeds $15,000,000 for a 2026 death, and tax applies only above that amount. IRS · Estate tax ↗IRS · Frequently asked questions on estate taxes ↗ On the modelled values, the two properties total $10,323,560 in 2045, leaving $13,819,960 of cash headroom before any federal tax could apply.

6. What would change the recommendation

  • The pending full-exclusion proposal. LAO's analysis of initiative 25-0017 describes restoring pre-Proposition 19 rules from 1 January 2027, applying to all inherited primary homes regardless of value and retroactively to transfers since February 2021. If enacted, renting A would keep the base year value and gain about $719,050 by 2055, enough to overtake selling. LAO · LAO analysis of proposed initiative A.G. File No.… ↗ It did not appear on the 3 November 2026 statewide ballot: the Secretary of State's official memorandum of 18 May 2026 records the petition's failure for insufficient signatures, so Proposition 19 rules remain in effect. CA SOS · Qualified Statewide Ballot Measures ↗CA SOS · CC/ROV Memorandum #26138 ↗
  • Sustained returns below about 6.5%. At a 4.0% reinvestment return, selling is the worst of the three hold-or-sell options. See the sensitivity matrix.
  • A decision to live in A long term. Then the exclusion plus avoided transaction costs make occupancy defensible.

7. Actions before 2040

  1. Confirm in writing how title is held, which trust holds each property, and whether both are community property — this decides whether there is one step-up or two.
  2. Ask the trustee to keep a current appraisal file and the trust's Schedule of assets (not published here).
  3. Obtain the actual annual insurance declarations and utility bills; every insurance figure in this edition is unlabelled-scenario or an owner estimate.
  4. Have counsel confirm the Proposition 19 claim calendar before, not after, the transfer.
